How the contest works

  1. Sign up

    By October 10th

    React or emote on the sign-up post to say you're in. Open to Wanted Alliance members only.

  2. Qualifying round

    October 17th, 8:00 PM Eastern

    Wear your costume at your guild Stronghold. Judges visit each Stronghold and pick who makes the finale. Takes about 1 hour.

  3. Live finale

    October 24th, 8:00 PM Eastern

    Finalists compete live on Twitch, hosted by Nolanian & Ivydora. Viewers vote for two community choice winners.

About the event

Calling all fashionistas, costume collectors, and masters of the masquerade! The Halloween Fashion Spectacular, presented by Synergy, is your chance to show off your most creative, frightening, or downright spectacular Halloween look and compete for a share of 50 Million Astral Diamonds and the exclusive "Frightfully Fashionable" title.

Start digging through those fashion collections, dyes, transmutes and masks. The Wanted Alliance is looking for its most Frightfully Fashionable adventurers.

Everyone watching can join the fun too. There are giveaway prizes throughout the stream, and viewers vote for two community choice winners. Make sure to tune in!

Before you sign up: you're committing to be available for both the October 17th qualifying round and, if selected, the October 24th live finale at 8:00 PM Eastern.

Questions? Ask in #general and tag @Ivydora and/or @Nolanian.
